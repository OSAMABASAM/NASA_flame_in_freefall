## Facts behind the lines
- Apollo 1 (1967): a cabin fire during a ground test killed its three-person crew.
- Fire behaves differently in low gravity: without buoyancy, hot gas does not rise, which is why NASA studies combustion in microgravity (challenge brief and NASA PSI records).
- Scattered and hard to compare: the challenge brief says the data is hard to find, compare and interpret.
- No experiment in our data ran in Moon gravity: computed from `data/investigations.json` in the Research gaps section.
